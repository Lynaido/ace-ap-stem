RESEND EMAIL SETUP GUIDE
========================

STEP 1: CREATE RESEND ACCOUNT
-----------------------------
1. Go to https://resend.com
2. Click "Sign Up" (free account - 100 emails/day)
3. Verify your email address


STEP 2: GET API KEY
-------------------
1. In Resend dashboard, click "API Keys" in left sidebar
2. Click "Create API Key"
3. Name: Railway Production
4. Permission: Full access
5. Click "Add"
6. COPY THE KEY (starts with re_) - you won't see it again!


STEP 3: ADD TO RAILWAY
----------------------
1. Go to Railway dashboard
2. Click your backend service
3. Go to "Variables" tab
4. Click "New Variable" and add:

   Variable Name: RESEND_API_KEY
   Value: re_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx (paste your key)

5. Also add (for testing):

   Variable Name: RESEND_FROM_EMAIL
   Value: onboarding@resend.dev


STEP 4: REMOVE OLD VARIABLES (CLEANUP)
--------------------------------------
Delete these variables from Railway if they exist:
- SMTP_HOST
- SMTP_USER
- SMTP_PASSWORD
- SMTP_PORT
- SMTP_SECURE
- SMTP_FROM_EMAIL


STEP 5: DEPLOY
--------------
Railway will auto-redeploy after adding variables.
Or click "Deploy" button manually.


STEP 6: VERIFY
--------------
Check Railway logs. You should see:
"Email service (Resend) is configured"

Then test forgot password - email should arrive in seconds!


FOR PRODUCTION (OPTIONAL - CUSTOM DOMAIN)
-----------------------------------------
To send from your own domain (e.g., noreply@aceapstem.com):
1. In Resend, go to "Domains"
2. Click "Add Domain"
3. Enter: aceapstem.com
4. Add the DNS records Resend shows to your domain
5. Wait for verification
6. Update Railway variable:
   RESEND_FROM_EMAIL = noreply@aceapstem.com
